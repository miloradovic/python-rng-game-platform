# Three Games. One Live Race to the Top.

Welcome to **Python RNG Game Platform**—a free-to-play arcade where every move
can shake up the weekly leaderboard. Spin for points, call the card, or prove
your memory under pressure. The rounds are quick, the results are immediate,
and the next run is always close.

No deposits. No wagers. No cash prizes. Just fast plays, collectible points,
and bragging rights on the board.

## Hourly Spin — give the wheel a rip

The wheel is ready. Hit **SPIN**, watch it fly, and lock up **10 or 50 points**
for your weekly score. A fresh shot lands every hour, so there is always another
chance to climb.

This is not a mystery-box result: each spin uses a cryptographic
commitment-and-reveal proof. Open the fairness details after the wheel stops and
verify the result independently. Clean spin, visible proof, points on the board.

`![Hourly Spin prize wheel, verified result, and live leaderboard](docs/images/about/hourly-spin.jpg)`

## Red or Black — make the call

Two colors. One decision. **Pick red or black**, reveal the server-owned card,
and bag **25 points** when your read lands. With a new call every minute, this is
the rapid-fire table for players who trust their gut and want one more shot at
the top 10.

Your choice is locked before the card comes back, and the recorded result—not
the browser—decides the score. Call it, flip it, collect it.

`![Red or Black card reveal and live leaderboard](docs/images/about/red-or-black.jpg)`

## Memory Rush — hold your nerve

Watch five numbers flash up. When they vanish, repeat the run in the exact same
order before the 30-second window shuts. Every correct number from the start of
the sequence pushes the score higher, all the way to a **perfect 1,000**.

Memory Rush is the skill table: no lucky bounce, just focus, recall, and the
pressure of knowing the weekly board is watching. Nail the sequence, post the
score, and dare the lobby to beat it. A new challenge is ready every 30 seconds.

`![Perfect Memory Rush score and live leaderboard](docs/images/about/memory-rush.jpg)`

## Every round feeds the rivalry

Each game has its own live weekly leaderboard, top-10 chase, personal-best rank,
and reset countdown. When the board moves, you see it move. If a connection
drops or a page reloads, completed plays and scores come back from the durable
record instead of disappearing.

Public player labels keep private display names off the leaderboard. Outcomes,
scores, cooldowns, and rewards are decided on the server, while safe retries
make sure a double-click or shaky connection cannot duplicate a result.

## Ready for a run?

Pick your table, take your shot, and put a score on the board. Then come back
before the weekly reset and defend your spot.
